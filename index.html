Daily Writing

A new prompt every day. Miss a day? Nothing happens — just pick it back up when you can.

Sign in with Google and each entry you save is appended to a Google Doc called “Daily Writing” in your own Drive — created for you on your first save.

Google sign-in is still being switched on for this page, so the button is inactive in this preview. Once the site’s Google client ID is added, sign-in and saving go live with no other changes.

Your entries save to your “Daily Writing” doc — it’s created on your first save.

Saved entries land in your Doc in the order you write them.

How it works

One prompt a day, rotating through gratitude, everyday journaling, a bit of fun, and small writing exercises. Write as much or as little as you like — there’s no target and no streak to keep.

Every saved entry is appended to the same “Daily Writing” doc, in this format:

How did your life change today? Monday, May 18, 2026 Your writing goes here, under the prompt it answers.

The doc lives in your own Google Drive, under your own account. This page never sees your password — Google handles the sign-in, and the page only gets permission to work with the doc it creates and your documents.
